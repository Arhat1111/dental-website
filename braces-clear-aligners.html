BRACES & CLEAR ALIGNERS IN SURAT

Straighten Your Teeth. Feel More Confident About Your Smile.

Image Dental Care & Implant Centre, Piplod, Surat. Treatment suitability is determined by an individual clinical assessment.

What Can Smile Correction Help With?

  • Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Certain bite irregularities
  • Overlapping or rotated teeth
  • Selected alignment concerns affecting smile appearance

Clear Aligners — A Discreet Option

Clear aligners are removable, transparent trays designed to gradually move teeth. They may suit people who prefer a less visible option and can follow the prescribed wear schedule.

Clear Aligner Benefits

  • Transparent appearance
  • Removable for eating and oral hygiene
  • No fixed brackets or wires
  • Suitable for selected alignment concerns

Dental Braces — A Time-Tested Approach

Braces use brackets and wires to move teeth into planned positions. They can help with a range of alignment and bite concerns depending on complexity.

Your Smile Correction Journey

  • Smile assessment and discussion of goals
  • Dental examination and imaging when needed
  • Personalised plan and estimated costs
  • Active treatment with monitoring
  • Retention and follow-up after treatment

Planning a Visit from Another City?

Orthodontic treatment often lasts several months or longer and requires ongoing monitoring. Discuss future reviews and adjustment arrangements before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are aligners better than braces?

Neither is best for everybody. The choice depends on complexity, lifestyle and compliance.

How long does treatment take?

Treatment ranges from months to years depending on the case.

Can adults get braces?

Yes, if teeth and gums are suitable following examination.

Will my teeth move back?

Retainers and follow-up help maintain alignment after treatment.

Can I begin if I live outside Surat?

You can enquire, but ongoing appointments must be planned before treatment.
