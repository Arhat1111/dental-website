WISDOM TOOTH REMOVAL IN SURAT

The Right Care for Problematic Wisdom Teeth.

Image Dental Care & Implant Centre, Piplod, Surat. Treatment suitability is determined by an individual clinical assessment.

When Might Treatment Be Needed?

  • Pain at the back of the mouth
  • Swelling or tenderness of gums
  • Repeated inflammation or infection
  • Food trapping and cleaning difficulties
  • Decay or damage to neighbouring teeth
  • Cysts or other pathology in selected cases

What Is Wisdom Tooth Surgery?

Surgical extraction removes a wisdom tooth when clinically indicated. Complexity depends on tooth position, roots and nearby structures. Your clinician may recommend imaging.

Recovery and Aftercare

Some discomfort, swelling and limited mouth opening can follow extraction. Recovery depends on individual healing and procedure complexity. Instructions cover hygiene, diet, medication and follow-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every wisdom tooth require removal?

No. Healthy teeth without problems may be monitored.

Will I need an X-ray?

Imaging may be needed to assess position, roots and nearby structures.

How long is recovery?

Recovery varies with surgical complexity and individual healing.
