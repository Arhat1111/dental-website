Restore Your Smile. Explore Your Tooth Replacement Options.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a fixture placed in the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or dental prosthesis.

Who May Be Suitable?

  • Jawbone volume and quality
  • Gum health and oral hygiene
  • General health and medications
  • Smoking status and healing considerations
  • Bite, neighbouring teeth and restorative needs

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation and assessment
  • Treatment planning and discussion of options
  • Implant placement where indicated
  • Healing and restoration
  • Maintenance and follow-up

Treatment Planning

Suitability, healing time and restoration stages vary by patient. An examination helps determine whether implants or alternatives are suitable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are implants suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability must be assessed for each patient.

How long does treatment take?

Depends on bone, gums, healing and additional procedures.

Are there alternatives?

Options may include a bridge or removable denture.
