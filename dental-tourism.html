Dental Tourism Assistance

  • Airport transfers can be coordinated subject to availability; patients pay transportation costs separately
  • Accommodation options may be discussed; accommodation is paid by patients
  • Appointments are coordinated around travel dates and clinical requirements
  • Cost estimates and treatment planning follow clinical assessment
  • Aftercare arrangements and future follow-up should be discussed before travelling

How to Plan Your Dental Visit

  • Send your enquiry and travel dates
  • Share available dental records, where appropriate
  • Attend clinical consultation
  • Coordinate stay and treatment stages
  • Plan follow-up and return visits

Important Considerations

Not all treatments can be completed in one trip. Orthodontics, implants and complex treatments may require several visits, healing or ongoing review. Confirm availability of services and travel assistance with the clinic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are airport transfers included?

No. Transfers may be coordinated subject to availability and are paid separately.

Is accommodation included?

No. Accommodation costs are borne by patients.

Can I know costs before travelling?

A preliminary estimate may be discussed, but final fees require clinical assessment.

Can all treatment finish in one trip?

Not always. Some treatments require multiple visits, healing or long-term follow-up.
