BRACES & CLEAR ALIGNERS

Straighten Your Teeth. Feel More Confident About Your Smile.

Thinking about correcting crooked teeth, gaps or an uneven smile? Our team offers consultations to help you explore orthodontic treatment options suited to your dental needs.

The first step is understanding your tooth alignment, bite and treatment goals. Treatment suitability depends on individual assessment.

SMILE CORRECTION

What can orthodontic treatment help with?

Crooked or crowded teeth Gaps between teeth Certain bite irregularities Overlapping or rotated teeth Selected smile alignment concerns
01 — DISCREET OPTION

Clear Aligners

Removable transparent trays designed to move teeth gradually according to a planned treatment sequence.

  • Transparent appearance
  • Removable for meals and oral hygiene
  • No fixed brackets or wires
  • Suitable for selected alignment concerns

Not appropriate for every case; results depend on clinical selection and following the wearing schedule.

02 — TIME-TESTED APPROACH

Dental Braces

Brackets and wires gradually move teeth into planned positions and may help with a range of alignment and bite concerns.

  • Useful for many types of alignment problems
  • Continuous action without removing an appliance for meals
  • Different options based on clinical suitability

Your dentist will explain brace options, expected duration and maintenance needs.

PERSONALISED GUIDANCE

Braces or Clear Aligners: Which Is Right for You?

The choice depends on your alignment, bite, treatment goals, budget and willingness to follow the treatment plan. A consultation can help you understand suitable options, limitations, expected timelines and care requirements.
