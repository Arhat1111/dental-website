What Is Smile Design?

A personalised approach to assessing aesthetic improvements, considering tooth colour, shape, alignment, proportions, gum appearance and oral health.

Cosmetic Dental Treatments

  • Professional teeth whitening: options to reduce some types of discolouration
  • Composite bonding: tooth-coloured material for selected chips, gaps and shape concerns
  • Dental veneers: thin front-surface restorations for selected teeth
  • Crowns and restorative dentistry: options when structural protection is also needed

Your Smile Design Journey

  • Understand your goals
  • Assess teeth, gums, bite and existing restorations
  • Explore options, limitations, maintenance and cost
  • Agree on a suitable treatment plan

Frequently Asked Questions

Is smile design suitable for everyone?

It depends on oral health and expectations. Gum or dental concerns may need treatment first.

Does smile design always require veneers?

No. Whitening, bonding, orthodontics, restorations or no procedure may be most appropriate.

How long does smile design take?

Timelines vary by treatment; orthodontics and complex restorations can take longer.
